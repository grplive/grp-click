The window uses GRP Live's brand fonts, Clash Display and General Sans (free from fontshare.com).
They aren't kept in this public repo. To build, put these files in `app/assets/fonts/`:
ClashDisplay-Medium.otf, ClashDisplay-Semibold.otf, GeneralSans-Regular.otf, GeneralSans-Medium.otf,
GeneralSans-Semibold.otf. Without them the window falls back to the system font.
